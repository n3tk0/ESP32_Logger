## v4.2.1 - Safe-mode + OTA rollback hardening
- [Breaking] RainSensor default pin moved from GPIO 9 → GPIO 10. GPIO 9
  is an ESP32-C3 bootstrap pin; a tipped bucket at power-on triggers SPI
  download mode (soft-brick). Existing deployments using the default
  pin MUST rewire to GPIO 10 OR explicitly set `"pin": 9` in
  platform_config.json (not recommended).
- [Fixed] Restart circuit breaker: 3 consecutive watchdog/panic resets
  without a 60-s healthy uptime now boots into AP-only safe mode for
  OTA recovery instead of looping until flash wears out (AUDIT FC.4).
- [Fixed] OTA auto-confirmation removed from restart / deep-sleep paths.
  A pending-verify image that crashes within 90 s now rolls back instead
  of being silently confirmed (AUDIT FC.1). Legacy-mode users may need
  to /api/ota/confirm manually if the device sleeps before 90 s uptime.
